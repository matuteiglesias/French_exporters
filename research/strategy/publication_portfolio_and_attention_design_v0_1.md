# Publication Portfolio and Attention Design v0.1

**State:** REVIEW  
**Date:** 2026-07-23  
**Programme:** Economics of Aggregation, Scale, and Measurement  
**Active front:** P2 — Concentration Is Not the Scaling Exponent

## Purpose

Design how one coherent research programme can generate papers that are genuinely different across audiences while relying on one common experimental and provenance infrastructure.

This is not a venue decision, submission plan, novelty claim, or authorization to draft several overlapping manuscripts. It is a portfolio map to prevent two opposite failures:

1. expressing a real contribution so cryptically that relevant researchers do not recognize why it matters;
2. repackaging the same scientific result as several nominally different papers.

## Governing principle

The invariant scientific core is:

> Concentration is a state variable at a given scale. The aggregate variance-decay exponent is a scale-dependent elasticity of diagonal and covariance contributions along a specified population path. Heterogeneous local variance laws can combine coherently at the aggregate level, but generally through changing contribution weights and covariance corrections rather than a fixed average.

The papers may differ in question, audience, estimand emphasis, empirical domain, and evidence package. They must not differ by silently changing the meaning of alpha, the population path, the partition, or the variance object.

# 1. Team formation

## Methodkeeper

Owns the invariant definitions:

- variance versus standard deviation;
- signed slope versus positive alpha;
- local part alpha versus aggregate effective alpha;
- population-path meaning;
- diagonal and covariance contributions;
- distinction between concentration level and scaling elasticity.

## Literature Scout

Maps each paper to the intellectual conversation it must enter. The scout records:

- the audience's familiar puzzle;
- claims already established;
- expected methods and evidence;
- likely misunderstanding of the contribution;
- terms the audience uses for closely related objects.

## Critic

Checks:

- whether a proposed paper has a distinct primary question;
- whether it owns a distinct claim or dataset;
- whether it overstates novelty;
- whether the first page makes the contribution visible;
- whether a journal fit requires mechanisms or evidence the project does not have.

## Collaboration Scout

After the central result is validated, maps researchers, seminars, workshops, and conference communities by audience. No outreach is part of this v0.1 play.

## Synthesizer / Finisher

Produces venue-specific paper profiles, but all profiles consume the same accepted scientific artifacts.

# 2. Integrated versus split publication architecture

The programme must choose one of two architectures after direct reproduction and the micro-to-macro reconstruction test.

## Route A — integrated flagship

One paper owns:

- the conceptual distinction between concentration and scaling;
- the local-to-global aggregation formula;
- controlled synthetic validation;
- the French-export empirical application;
- the direct-versus-reconstructed alpha test.

This is the default while evidence volume is still uncertain.

## Route B — two-paper split

This is permitted only if each manuscript can stand independently and the evidence supports a real division.

### B1. Formal aggregation paper

Owns:

- the general theorem or local identity;
- existence and sign conditions;
- covariance-adjusted aggregation;
- finite-scale versus asymptotic behavior;
- analytic and synthetic experiments.

The empirical export application is illustrative rather than the paper's main result.

### B2. Trade-volatility paper

Owns:

- the empirical export-diversification question;
- direct export alpha;
- empirical part and covariance contributions;
- the direct-versus-reconstructed comparison;
- trade interpretation and policy relevance.

It cites the formal result rather than reproducing the formal paper in full.

## Prohibited architecture

Do not submit an integrated flagship and a split formal or trade paper containing substantially the same central evidence and claim. Candidate profiles may coexist during research design; overlapping manuscripts may not.

# 3. Candidate paper profiles

## Profile F — Integrated flagship

### Working title

**Concentration Is Not the Scaling Exponent: Heterogeneous Firms and Aggregate Volatility**

### Familiar puzzle

Economists frequently infer aggregate exposure from concentration. But a concentration measure describes weights at one scale, whereas volatility reduction depends on how variance and covariance contributions change as population scale changes.

### Primary question

How do heterogeneous local fluctuation laws and covariance combine into the effective volatility-scaling behavior of an aggregate population?

### Claim ladder

1. Concentration and volatility scaling are distinct objects.
2. Aggregate alpha is a contribution-weighted local elasticity with signed covariance corrections.
3. The direct aggregate exponent can be reconstructed from part-level contributions under a common population path.
4. French exporters provide an empirical test of the identity.

### Minimum evidence

- accepted notation and estimand contract;
- analytic derivation;
- deterministic synthetic checks;
- direct empirical export alpha;
- common-N component panel;
- diagonal-only and full reconstruction;
- adversarial population-path and covariance checks.

### Natural audience

Industrial dynamics, evolutionary economics, structural change, heterogeneous-firm macro and trade researchers.

### Best current venue family

- Industrial and Corporate Change;
- Journal of Evolutionary Economics;
- Structural Change and Economic Dynamics.

### Desk-risk

The paper will appear abstract or niche if it leads with alpha notation rather than the economic inference error: using concentration to infer stabilization.

---

## Profile D — Formal dynamics and aggregation

### Working title

**Aggregating Heterogeneous Variance-Decay Laws**

Alternative attention-oriented title:

**When Local Diversification Laws Do Not Average Out**

### Primary question

Under what conditions do heterogeneous local variance and covariance components generate a stable aggregate scaling exponent?

### Main objects

- local elasticities;
- signed covariance contributions;
- finite-range secant estimates;
- asymptotic dominance;
- moment-existence and truncation conditions;
- population-path invariance.

### Minimum evidence

- theorem or exact local identity;
- special cases;
- counterexamples to fixed weighted averages;
- simulations covering covariance regimes, heavy tails, and composition drift;
- one empirical illustration.

### Best current venue family

- Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control;
- Journal of Evolutionary Economics;
- Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization;
- Physica A only when the statistical-mechanics foundation is explicit and substantive.

### Desk-risk

A purely algebraic decomposition will be judged insufficient. The paper needs nontrivial implications, counterexamples, testable conditions, and a demonstrated empirical use.

---

## Profile T — International trade and export resilience

### Working title

**When Does Export Diversification Stabilize Aggregate Exports?**

Alternative:

**Firm Heterogeneity, Concentration, and Aggregate Export Volatility**

### Familiar puzzle

Countries and sectors are often called resilient or fragile from export concentration measures. Yet two export populations with similar concentration can exhibit different volatility scaling because their firm-level moments and covariance structures differ.

### Primary question

When does adding exporters reduce aggregate export volatility, and when is apparent diversification offset by common movement or composition change?

### Main empirical contribution

- direct export variance decay;
- local bin laws;
- covariance contribution;
- comparison of concentration-based predictions with realized scaling;
- economically interpretable decomposition of stabilization failure.

### Minimum evidence for a leading trade outlet

- a clearly trade-specific mechanism rather than a generic aggregation exercise;
- a credible population expansion or resampling path;
- replication-ready code and data boundary;
- evidence beyond a single decorative coefficient;
- preferably cross-sector, destination, product, time-regime, or comparative-country variation;
- policy interpretation tied to export diversification or resilience.

### Venue ladder

Stretch:

- Journal of International Economics.

Strong field alternatives:

- International Economics;
- Review of International Economics;
- The World Economy.

### Desk-risk

A France-only historical recovery with no broader trade mechanism will look narrow. The trade version must make export diversification, common shocks, and resilience—not alpha itself—the organizing question.

---

## Profile S — Structural composition and macro instability

### Working title

**Composition, Covariance, and Aggregate Volatility in Evolving Firm Populations**

### Primary question

When does a measured scaling exponent represent diversification, and when does it mix diversification with selection, entry, exit, or changing composition?

### Main contribution

- distinguish population-scale change from composition drift;
- show how selection changes local moments and covariance;
- connect weak market selection and persistent heterogeneity to aggregate instability;
- interpret alpha as a path-dependent reduced-form elasticity unless invariance conditions hold.

### Best current venue family

- Structural Change and Economic Dynamics;
- Industrial and Corporate Change;
- Journal of Evolutionary Economics.

### Required extension

This profile needs explicit entry/exit, segment transition, sectoral composition, or regime evidence. It cannot be created only by changing the introduction of Profile F.

---

## Profile R — Innovation or financial-regime extension

### Example title

**Innovation Regimes and the Scaling of Aggregate Firm Volatility**

or

**Financial Constraints and the Aggregation of Firm Growth Shocks**

### Primary question

Do persistent innovation or financial states alter aggregate volatility because they change local moments, population paths, or covariance structures?

### Potential venue family

- Research Policy for a genuinely innovation-centered mechanism with management or policy implications;
- Industrial and Corporate Change;
- Journal of Evolutionary Economics;
- Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization.

### Current status

FUTURE EXTENSION. The existing French-export front does not yet establish the data or mechanism required for this paper.

### Prohibition

Do not cite innovation or financial heterogeneity as a venue hook unless it enters the measured micro moments, covariance, or population transition process.

# 4. Journal-audience map

## Industrial and Corporate Change

### Why the agenda fits

ICC explicitly covers corporate organization and change, industrial dynamics, innovation, productivity and growth, and accepts varied methods when theory is sound and implications are clear.

### Best paper interface

Profile F or S.

### What to foreground

- persistent firm heterogeneity;
- why observed micro regularities do not mechanically imply aggregate behavior;
- consequences for industrial dynamics and interpretation of firm populations;
- transparent empirical mechanism.

### What not to foreground first

A notation-heavy derivation detached from firms, industries, and economic implications.

## Journal of Evolutionary Economics

### Why the agenda fits

JEE emphasizes dynamics, changing structures, disequilibrium, industrial dynamics, complex dynamic economic systems, and theoretical, methodological, and applied evolutionary work.

### Best paper interface

Profile F, D, or S.

### What to foreground

- heterogeneous populations;
- changing structures and composition;
- finite-scale rather than equilibrium-only behavior;
- selection and covariance as evolving mechanisms.

## Structural Change and Economic Dynamics

### Why the agenda fits

SCED welcomes theoretical, empirical, historical, and methodological work on dynamics and structural change, including complex and dynamical analysis.

### Best paper interface

Profile S or an integrated F with substantial sectoral/composition evidence.

### What to foreground

- structural composition;
- path dependence of measured alpha;
- macro instability emerging from micro populations;
- sectoral or regime variation.

## Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control

### Why the agenda fits

JEDC covers theoretical and empirical economic dynamics and computational methods.

### Best paper interface

Profile D.

### What to foreground

- exact dynamic identity;
- finite-scale estimators;
- computational implementation;
- simulation and empirical discrimination among regimes.

## Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization

### Why the agenda conditionally fits

JEBO welcomes theoretical, empirical, simulation, and mathematical work on organizations, market economies, structural features, and micro-macro behavior.

### Best paper interface

Profile D or R when organizational heterogeneity is a measured mechanism.

### Main risk

An export-variance decomposition without behavioral or organizational interpretation may sit outside the journal's strongest audience.

## Journal of International Economics

### Why it is a stretch target

JIE is a primary outlet for theoretical and empirical international economics, expects original motivation or modelling, and requires replication materials.

### Best paper interface

Profile T only.

### Entry condition

The paper must change how international economists interpret export diversification, granularity, common shocks, or trade resilience. A generic scaling result with exports as an application is insufficient.

## International Economics / Review of International Economics / The World Economy

### Why they are strong field alternatives

These journals publish applied, theoretical, empirical, and policy-relevant international economics. The World Economy particularly rewards succinct, clearly defined, accessible trade questions.

### Best paper interface

Profile T.

### Accessibility requirement

Replace “estimate alpha” in the opening with “identify when adding exporters actually stabilizes national exports.”

## Physica A

### Why it conditionally fits

Physica A explicitly requires statistical-mechanics concepts and welcomes micro-meso-macro economic systems, econophysics, and complex systems.

### Best paper interface

Profile D only when the work genuinely uses statistical-mechanics reasoning rather than ordinary econometrics with scaling language.

### Main risk

An economic or econometric paper that merely plots power laws is explicitly outside its preferred scope.

## Research Policy

### Why current fit is weak

Research Policy centers on science, technology, innovation, and implications for policy or management.

### Entry condition

Use Profile R only after innovation processes materially determine the aggregation mechanism and the results have clear innovation-policy or management implications.

## General-interest economics journals

The Economic Journal and JEEA are aspirational only after the contribution becomes broad enough to matter outside industrial dynamics and trade.

A credible general-interest version would require at least one of:

- a sharp general theorem with wide economic consequences;
- several empirical domains;
- a major reinterpretation of diversification or aggregate volatility;
- a strong policy or welfare implication.

Do not plan a general-interest submission from the current recovered coefficient alone.

# 5. Attention architecture

The programme should be mathematically deep but rhetorically front-loaded.

## Universal five-sentence pitch

1. Economists often use concentration to infer how exposed an aggregate is to firm-level shocks.
2. That inference is incomplete because concentration is a snapshot, while stabilization is a scaling property along a population path.
3. We derive the aggregate volatility exponent from the changing contributions of heterogeneous groups and their covariance.
4. We test whether the directly measured aggregate exponent can be reconstructed from those components in firm-level export data.
5. The result identifies when diversification works, when common movement defeats it, and when a single reported exponent mixes diversification with composition change.

## First-figure requirement

Figure 1 should communicate the distinction without requiring notation.

Preferred alternatives:

- two populations with similar concentration but different volatility-decay paths;
- direct aggregate alpha versus diagonal-only and covariance-adjusted reconstruction;
- a decomposition showing which component prevents ordinary `1/N` stabilization.

## Title rule

Lead with the economic puzzle or consequence. Put the technical object second.

Better:

- **When Does Export Diversification Stabilize Aggregate Exports?**
- **Concentration Is Not the Scaling Exponent**
- **When Local Diversification Laws Do Not Average Out**

Weaker:

- **A Covariance-Adjusted Contribution-Weighted Effective Elasticity of Export Variance**

## Abstract rule

The abstract should contain, in order:

1. familiar inference or puzzle;
2. why it is wrong or incomplete;
3. the formal resolution;
4. the decisive empirical test;
5. the economic consequence.

Do not open with notation, notebook provenance, or the recovered value near 0.48.

## Presentation ladder

Every public version should support three levels:

### Level 1 — thirty seconds

Concentration tells us who holds the weight. It does not tell us how volatility falls when more firms enter. That depends on micro moments and common movement.

### Level 2 — three minutes

Show the first figure and explain direct versus reconstructed aggregate alpha.

### Level 3 — technical

Introduce population paths, local elasticities, signed covariance terms, and estimator contracts.

# 6. One infrastructure, several paper views

The experimental system should produce canonical scientific artifacts once. Papers select evidence; they do not create incompatible computations.

## Canonical artifacts

- run manifests;
- scenario tables;
- component panels;
- estimate panels;
- check results;
- accepted figures with data hashes.

A paper renderer may change ordering, labels, explanatory text, and selected figures. It must not change the underlying result values or scientific definitions.

## Claim ownership

Every claim has one owner paper.

Other papers may:

- cite the owner;
- use the result as an input;
- test it in a distinct domain;
- extend it with a new mechanism.

They may not present the same evidence as an independent new central contribution.

# 8. Immediate decisions and non-decisions

## Accepted as programme strategy

- one common experimental infrastructure should serve all paper profiles;
- papers should differ by primary question and evidence, not by relabeling;
- the first public interface should lead with diversification and aggregate volatility, not unexplained alpha notation;
- ICC, JEE, SCED, JEDC, and trade-field journals represent distinct plausible audiences;
- venue selection remains downstream of empirical validation.

## Remains unresolved

- integrated flagship versus formal/trade split;
- final central venue;
- novelty relative to granularity and diversification literatures;
- whether the empirical evidence is broad enough for a leading trade or general-interest journal;
- whether innovation, finance, or structural-composition extensions have adequate data.

# 9. Exact next pointer

Do not begin venue-specific manuscripts.

Complete the active lineage and reproduction gates, then revisit this artifact with:

1. the validated direct-versus-reconstructed result;
